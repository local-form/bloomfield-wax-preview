      Your first visit

      Nothing to be nervous about. Here's the whole thing.

      1. 1

        Before

        • Book online or call. We call to confirm 24–48 hours ahead.
        • Let the hair grow to about 1/4 inch, roughly two weeks without shaving.
        • Skip waxing with a sunburn, or while you use Retin-A, Renova or Accutane.
        • Come on your own: no extra guests or children during your appointment.
      2. 2

        On the table

        • Reviews say it again and again: your specialist walks you through it and doesn't rush.
        • Brazilian? An ibuprofen 30 minutes before may help. On your period you can still come in with a clean tampon.
        • Prefer a natural option? Ask for sugar wax, our organic menu.

        “There was no rush & she was so gentle.” Ebony

      3. 3

        After

        • No oils or creams except the homecare your specialist gives you.
        • No tanning for about 48 hours. Waxing exfoliates, so your skin is extra sensitive.
        • Prone to ingrown hairs? Ask about the PFB Vanish serum.

      Need to cancel? Give us more than 24 hours so someone on the waitlist can take the slot. Full policy
